# Troostkatten — 9 beeldprompts

Sla elke afbeelding op als `cards/kat-1.jpg` t/m `cards/kat-9.jpg` (zelfde nummer als hieronder).
Naam en flavor text staan al in `supabase/troostkatten-9.sql`.

**Tips voor alle prompts**
- Staand formaat **5:7** (bv. 1000×1400 px), net als de andere kaarten.
- Bovenaan (±15%) en onderaan (±25%) komen de naam en de voettekst over de afbeelding: hou de kat en de belangrijkste actie in het midden.
- Vraag altijd om **geen tekst of letters** in de afbeelding. AI-tekst ziet er meestal slecht uit.

### 1. Could've Been an Email
> A chubby grey tabby cat in a tiny business suit and tie stands at the head of a long glass boardroom table, pointing seriously with a laser pointer at a projector screen showing a bar chart made of fish. The other chairs are all occupied by empty cardboard boxes that are clearly asleep, with little "zzz" bubbles. Afternoon light through blinds, stale coffee cups everywhere. **Style: glossy 3D animated feature film, Pixar-like, soft global illumination, expressive faces, vibrant colours.** Portrait 5:7, subject centred, no text or letters.

### 2. Reply All
> A panicked orange cat sits on a computer keyboard, paw still on the Enter key, while a gigantic wave made of thousands of paper envelopes erupts out of the monitor and curls over an open-plan office. Tiny office plants and desk chairs are swept along in the wave; the cat has accidentally become a surfer on a mouse pad. **Style: Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock print, in the spirit of Hokusai's Great Wave, flat indigo and cream palette, visible wood grain texture.** Portrait 5:7, no text or letters.

### 3. The Printer Exorcist
> A solemn black cat in a priest's collar holds up a glowing USB stick like a cross towards a possessed office printer. The printer is levitating, spewing paper in a spiral, its display glowing demonic red. Candles are arranged around it on the carpet tiles, and a frightened houseplant hides behind a water cooler. **Style: dramatic Renaissance oil painting, Caravaggio chiaroscuro, rich dark shadows, golden candlelight, visible brush strokes.** Portrait 5:7, no text or letters.

### 4. Out of Office
> A relaxed white cat with sunglasses lies on a tiny striped beach lounger placed on top of an office desk, under a desk lamp that acts as a blazing sun. A little paper umbrella sticks out of a coffee mug turned into a cocktail, sand is spilled over the keyboard, and an inflatable flamingo floats in a water cooler. Everyone else's desks in the background are buried in paperwork. **Style: 1950s vintage travel poster, bold flat shapes, limited warm palette, subtle paper grain and halftone.** Portrait 5:7, no text or letters.

### 5. The Coffee Machine Cult
> A circle of cats wearing tiny hooded robes kneel in worship before an office coffee machine that glows like a holy relic on a pedestal, rays of light beaming from it. One cat carries an incense burner made from a paper cup, another holds a sacred sugar cube aloft. Decorative border of coffee beans and paw prints. **Style: medieval illuminated manuscript, gold leaf details, flat perspective, ornate margins, parchment texture.** Portrait 5:7, no text or letters.

### 6. Swivel Chair Hyperdrive
> A wide-eyed Siamese cat clings to a spinning office swivel chair that is going so fast it has launched into hyperspace, with star streaks, sticky notes and pens orbiting around it like debris. The office floor tiles stretch into a neon grid towards the horizon. **Style: 1980s synthwave / retrowave, neon pink and cyan, chrome highlights, glowing grid, retro sunset gradient.** Portrait 5:7, no text or letters.

### 7. The Stapler Heist
> Two cats in black turtlenecks and tiny night-vision goggles are lowered on ropes from an office ceiling panel, reaching for a single red stapler displayed on a pedestal inside a glass case, surrounded by a web of laser beams. One cat nervously holds its breath; a security camera looks the other way. **Style: black-and-white film noir, high-contrast lighting, venetian-blind shadows, the stapler is the only thing in colour (bright red).** Portrait 5:7, no text or letters.

### 8. Quarterly Targets
> A colossal fluffy ginger cat towers over a miniature city made of office cubicles, calmly pushing a giant trophy and a stack of binders off the edge of a skyscraper-sized desk. Tiny office workers flee in panic, a toppled bar chart lies in the street like a fallen tower. **Style: retro 1960s Japanese kaiju movie poster, painted illustration, dramatic low angle, saturated reds and yellows, slightly worn print texture.** Portrait 5:7, no text or letters.

### 9. Cardboard Corner Office
> A proud tuxedo cat sits behind a tiny mahogany desk inside a cardboard box that has been converted into a luxurious corner office: a miniature leather chair, a framed picture of a fish, a potted plant, a tiny window cut into the cardboard showing a city skyline. The box sits on the floor of an ordinary office, and a confused human shoe is visible at the edge. **Style: claymation / stop-motion, handmade plasticine textures, visible fingerprints, warm studio lighting, shallow depth of field.** Portrait 5:7, no text or letters.
